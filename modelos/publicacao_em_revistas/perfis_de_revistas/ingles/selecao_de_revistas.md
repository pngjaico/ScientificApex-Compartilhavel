# Journal selection

Six dated preparation candidates. No acceptance probability is established. Fit assessments are editorial judgments, not journal promises. These subscription routes avoid an APC; they are not confirmed free open access. Optional OA, print color, offprints or services can cost money. Reconfirm every charge and current category before submission. Templates never authorize payment.

- [Journal of Medical Systems](https://link.springer.com/journal/10916/submission-guidelines): Clinical systems/informatics with rigorous evaluation and practical relevance. [NLM](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nlmcatalog/7806056); [fees/taxas](https://link.springer.com/journal/10916/how-to-publish-with-us).
- [Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing](https://link.springer.com/journal/11517/submission-guidelines): Methodological innovation and clinical impact; routine public-dataset applications may be desk-rejected. [NLM](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nlmcatalog/7704869); [fees/taxas](https://link.springer.com/journal/11517/how-to-publish-with-us).
- [Plastic Surgery (Oakville)](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/PSG): Clinical plastic-surgery research/reviews; PubMed/PMC coverage, not currently MEDLINE-indexed. [NLM](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nlmcatalog?term=2292-5503%5BISSN%5D); [fees/taxas](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/PSG).
- [Aesthetic Plastic Surgery](https://link.springer.com/journal/266/submission-guidelines): Aesthetic surgery with demonstrated clinical contribution. Limits in the 2026 PDF remain unextracted; do not assume them. [NLM](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nlmcatalog/7701756); [fees/taxas](https://link.springer.com/journal/266/how-to-publish-with-us).
- [Surgery Today](https://link.springer.com/journal/595/submission-guidelines): Clinical/experimental surgical investigation; focused letters and practical techniques in their respective categories. [NLM](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nlmcatalog/9204360); [fees/taxas](https://link.springer.com/journal/595/how-to-publish-with-us).
- [Updates in Surgery](https://link.springer.com/journal/13304/submission-guidelines): Surgical research/synthesis; limits are characters, not words. Check multicenter policies. [NLM](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nlmcatalog/101539818); [fees/taxas](https://link.springer.com/journal/13304/how-to-publish-with-us).

Excluded from the free shortlist: Revista do Colégio Brasileiro de Cirurgiões has a mandatory publication charge in its [official instructions](https://revistadocbc.org.br/instructions). Elsevier candidates remain secondary until journal-specific fees/extras are accessible and verified. No diamond OA journal was confirmed in this shortlist; this does not establish that none exists. Aesthetic Plastic Surgery has a confirmed no-APC route, but its current PDF limits and extras remain pending.
