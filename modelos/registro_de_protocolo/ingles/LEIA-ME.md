# Protocol and registration templates

Complete questionario.md with the team, use fluxo_de_decisao.md to choose a method/registry, and follow guia_de_registro_e_verificacao.md. The same filenames appear in the ingles directory; filenames remain in Portuguese. Each document is written natively in its language.

modelo_registro_em_branco.json is deliberately invalid for verification: it has no ID, URL, dates, hash, reviewer, or confirmation, and `scientific_work_not_started_before_registration` is `false`. It is not a real registration, must not be submitted, and must remain blocked until the team completes the external registration and reviews real evidence. Never copy an example ID into a protocol. The reviewer may attest that scientific work had not started only after reconciling legacy sources and activity outside the pipeline; missing markers/manifests do not prove that no work occurred.

Preparation and freeze may happen during bootstrap/protocol-freeze. For scientific projects, local policy requires a reviewed final PROSPERO/OSF registration before corpus building, formal search/screening, extraction, analysis, discovery, or writing. The local registry register command only declares a registration already completed; registry verify checks locally stored evidence. Neither command creates an external registration.

The service checks local consistency and hashes; it does not automatically authenticate with the provider. Keep a dated snapshot, frozen protocol, fingerprint, authorship, ethics, version, and amendments. Check PROSPERO eligibility and current OSF options directly against official sources.
