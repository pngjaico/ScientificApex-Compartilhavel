# Paper To Talk

Translate a paper into a spoken narrative.

## When To Use
- The source is a manuscript, draft, systematic review, NMA, thesis chapter, or claim map.
- The target is an oral congress presentation or seminar.

## Contract
- Start from the research question and main claim-safe findings.
- Compress methods to what the audience needs to trust the result.
- Preserve uncertainty, bias, and limitations.
- Generate `storyboard.yaml` through `article-machine deck plan`.

## Shape
- Problem.
- Design.
- Main finding.
- Interpretation.
- Limitation.
- Next step.
