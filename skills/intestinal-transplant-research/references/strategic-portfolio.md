# Strategic Portfolio

This reference summarizes the imported 2026 strategic report. Treat it as a planning map, not verified evidence.

## Strongest Near-Term Publication Lanes

- Brazilian SUS implementation after 2025 intestinal and multivisceral transplant incorporation: high immediate policy/epidemiology potential if primary official sources and national data can be verified.
- Brazilian multicenter TID/TMV cohort: high value if collaboration and chart access exist.
- SRTR/OPTN and NHSBT registry studies: useful because the field is rare, but statistical fragility and graft heterogeneity are real defects.
- Non-invasive rejection biomarkers: open field; dd-cfDNA is emerging, but likely too sparse for broad quantitative pooling.
- Preclinical intestinal machine perfusion meta-analysis: plausible because the human clinical base is not mature.
- GLP-2 living review/NMA: possible only with careful subgroups; simple teduglutide efficacy meta-analysis is probably saturated.

## Strongest Doctoral/Signature Lanes

- Intestinal perfusion with perfusate/tissue omics and viability biomarkers.
- Integrated rejection monitoring: dd-cfDNA, citrulline, calprotectin, histology, and endoscopy.
- Organoid or enteroid I/R repair platform with omics.
- Digital pathology for intestinal graft biopsy.
- Mechanism-guided drug repurposing for epithelial barrier and intestinal I/R.

## Low-Value Or High-Risk Traps

- Another small I/R animal study with only histology, TNF, IL-6, and MDA.
- A generic ML model on rare intestinal-transplant data without calibration, external validation, or small-n humility.
- A pediatric SBS microbiome review that repeats the 2025 synthesis instead of moving to adult, multiomics, anatomy, or GLP-2 response.
- Treating organoid engraftment or ex vivo perfusion findings as near-clinical proof.
- Mixing isolated intestine, liver-containing, and multivisceral grafts without stratification.

## Venue Heuristics

- TTS, ATC, ESOT: transplant-heavy work, registry studies, perfusion, biomarkers, immunology, digital transformation.
- CIIRTA: intestinal failure, SBS, GLP-2, rehabilitation, intestinal transplant.
- DDW and UEG: organoids, microbiome, GI translational work, SBS.
- ILTS: perfusion and liver-containing/multivisceral interfaces.
- ABTO: Brazilian implementation, local series, national network, service workflow.

## Journal Heuristics

- Transplant-focused: American Journal of Transplantation, Transplantation, Transplant International, Clinical Transplantation.
- Intestinal failure/SBS/GLP-2: Intestinal Failure, JPEN, Clinical Nutrition.
- Pediatric intestinal transplant: Pediatric Transplantation, Intestinal Failure.
- GI translational: UEG Journal, Gastroenterology/GIE ecosystem when aligned.
- Brazilian implementation: Brazilian Journal of Transplantation can be strategically better than premature international overreach.
