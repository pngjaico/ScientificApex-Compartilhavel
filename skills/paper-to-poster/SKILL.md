# Paper To Poster

Translate a paper into a scan-friendly poster.

## When To Use
- The source is a manuscript, review, NMA, thesis chapter, or claim map.
- The target is a vertical or horizontal congress poster.

## Contract
- Prioritize title, question, methods, 3 to 5 findings, conclusion, QR payload, authors, and affiliations.
- Keep blocks short and readable at print size.
- Use only supported claim IDs.
- Generate via `article-machine poster plan`, `build`, `qa`, and `export`.

## Poster Rule
If a paragraph cannot be read in under 20 seconds, compress it.
